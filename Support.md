# Mighty Printer — Support

For help with Mighty Printer, to report a problem, or to suggest an improvement, email:

**[mightypdf@protonmail.com](mailto:mightypdf@protonmail.com)**

We suggest using a subject line such as **Mighty Printer — Support Request**.

## What to include in your request

To help us understand the issue, please include the following where possible:

- Your device model and iOS version.
- Your Mighty Printer version.
- A description of the issue, steps to reproduce it, and any error message.
- What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
- For printing issues, your printer’s make and model, paper size, and selected print mode.
- A screenshot, if helpful, with any personal information redacted.

Do not send passwords or confidential documents. If the issue involves a particular file, describe it or use a sample document that contains no personal information.

## Quick help

**Your printer does not appear**

Check that it supports AirPrint, is turned on, and is reachable on the same network as your device.

**Your printout is scaled or cropped**

Check that the project’s paper size matches the paper size set on your printer. **Actual size** preserves dimensions, but content outside the printable area may be cropped. **Fit to printable area** scales the content to fit within the margins supported by your printer.

**The camera or scanner does not work**

Check in iOS Settings that Mighty Printer has permission to use the camera.

**You cannot find a saved project**

Open **My Prints** or look for the Mighty Printer folder on your device in the **Files** app. Exporting a PDF does not replace saving the editable project.
