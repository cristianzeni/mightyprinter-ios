
# Mighty Printer — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 4, 2026**

Mighty Printer lets you capture photos and scans, arrange images and PDFs, save projects, and print using AirPrint. This policy explains how data is handled when you use the app and contact support.

For privacy questions, you can contact the developer of Mighty Printer, identified in the app’s App Store listing, at [mightypdf@protonmail.com](mailto:mightypdf@protonmail.com).

## Data handled by the app

Mighty Printer processes photos, scans, PDFs, and projects directly on your device. This content is not sent to the developer’s servers, and the developer cannot access your library through the app.

The app does not require an account, display advertisements, integrate analytics or tracking tools, or collect personal data for advertising or profiling. It does not sell your data.

## Photos, camera, and documents

- **Camera:** access is requested when you choose to take a photo or scan a document. You can revoke permission in iOS Settings; features that require the camera will remain unavailable until you grant permission again.
- **Photos:** the app uses the system photo picker and receives only the images you choose to import, without requesting full access to your photo library.
- **Files:** the app accesses documents you choose to open or import using system features.

Saved projects include copies of source files, which may retain their original metadata. An imported PDF may be stored in full within a project, even if you use only some of its pages in your layout. To share only the finished layout, export the final PDF instead of the editable project.

Passwords entered to open protected PDFs are kept in session memory and are neither saved in the project nor sent to the developer. An exported PDF is a new document and does not retain the original PDF’s password protection.

## Printing, exporting, and external services

When you start a print job, Apple’s printing system sends the document to your selected printer using AirPrint. When you export or share a file, its content is made available to the destination or app you choose.

If you select content stored in iCloud or other services connected to Photos or Files, the system may download it from the relevant provider. Mighty Printer does not provide its own cloud synchronization service. Data handling by Apple, storage providers, destination apps, and printer services is governed by their respective privacy policies and your settings.

## Retention and deletion

Projects and PDFs saved in the app’s library remain on your device until you delete them. You can manage them in **My Prints** or in the Mighty Printer folder on your device using the **Files** app.

The app also uses working copies, drafts, and temporary files to support editing, exporting, and printing. Drafts and temporary files are excluded from backups; saved documents may be included in device backups according to your settings.

Deleting the app and its data lets you remove any remaining local app data. This does not delete originals in Photos or Files, exported or shared copies, or backups. Those copies must be managed separately in their respective locations.

## Email support

If you email [mightypdf@protonmail.com](mailto:mightypdf@protonmail.com), the developer receives your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and provide support, on the basis that it is necessary to deliver the service you requested, and is not used for promotional activities.

Communications are retained for as long as necessary to handle your request and any follow-up, unless a longer retention period is required by law. The email service used is Proton Mail. Please refer to its [privacy policy](https://proton.me/mail/privacy-policy) for information about how the provider handles data.

Send only the information needed for your request and redact any personal information in screenshots. Do not send passwords or confidential documents.

## Your privacy requests

For information you send to support, you may request access, correction, or deletion, as well as restriction of processing, objection to processing, and data portability where provided by applicable law, by emailing [mightypdf@protonmail.com](mailto:mightypdf@protonmail.com). You may also contact the competent data protection supervisory authority.

For documents stored exclusively on your device, you can manage them directly through the app and Files. The developer does not hold a remote copy that can be retrieved or deleted.
